Privacy Policy

What we store. Your profile name and contact, likes, chats and activity are stored in your browser (localStorage). When cloud mode is on, posts you publish and an anonymous account ID are stored on our database (Supabase).

Uploads. Videos you upload are hosted by Cloudinary and are public to anyone with the link.

Calls. Voice/video calls are peer-to-peer (PeerJS/WebRTC); your IP address is visible to the other party and to the signalling server.

Third parties. Google Fonts, Cloudinary, Supabase, PeerJS, YouTube embeds.

Advertising. If ads are enabled, advertising partners may use cookies to show and measure ads. Ads load only after you tap Accept; you can Decline or clear site data.
